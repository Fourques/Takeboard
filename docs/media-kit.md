# TakeBoard · 媒体素材

更新：2026-10-16。由项目维护者提供。

## 一句话介绍

**TakeBoard：基于 ComfyUI 的开源 AI 创作画布。**

把 AI 图片与视频创作，放进同一张画布。选择模型、连接参考素材、写下提示词，生成之后，用结果继续下一步。保留不同方向，让想法逐渐成为画面。

桌面 Beta，支持自己的本机或远程 ComfyUI。本机使用无需注册；ComfyUI、模型与节点需另行准备。

## English introduction

**TakeBoard is an open-source AI creation canvas built on ComfyUI.**

Bring models, reference media and prompts into one workspace. Generate images and videos, explore different directions side by side, then use a result to build the next scene.

Desktop beta for macOS, Windows and Debian/Ubuntu. Connect your own local or remote ComfyUI. Local use needs no account; ComfyUI, models and nodes are separate prerequisites. The app interface is currently primarily Chinese.

> A starting frame. A result worth keeping. A new scene to build. TakeBoard brings AI image and video creation into one open-source canvas, powered by your own ComfyUI. Watch the 42-second film: https://fourques.github.io/Takeboard/#demo
